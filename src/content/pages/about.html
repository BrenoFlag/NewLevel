NuLevel Group, a public relations agency specializing in public relations, communications, and digital marketing within the Luxury Weddings industry.

Founded by Kelli Webb in 2014, NuLevel Group initially focused on public relations services for the Chicago and Atlanta entertainment sectors and small businesses. By emphasizing consistency, we have expanded our services to clients nationwide and internationally, adding architecture and design firms and special events to our client roster.

We believe that creative strategic PR, original campaigns tailored to each client, thought-provoking marketing, organized execution, and business development require smart solutions and strong follow-through to succeed.

We pride ourselves on understanding, achieving, and striving to surpass our clients’ needs. Our team has many years of experience in public relations, marketing, and branding. Every client partnership is personal — driven by collaboration, precision, and a shared commitment to excellence.
