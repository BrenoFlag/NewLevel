Our Process

Simple easy steps to get started

  • Inquiry

    After deciding if your brand is the perfect fit for our team, we’ll send you a link to book a call with us.

    Your call is an opportunity to discuss your goals and how our team can properly support your journey.

  • Onboarding

    If we’re on the same page, you’ll receive onboarding materials, including a proposal, welcome guide, and invoice. Your onboarding process includes a Brand Storage Overview - our unique questionnaire that’s pertinent in developing your media pitches.

  • Phase 1

    PREPPING YOUR PUBLICITY CAMPAIGN

    We’re taking an extensive look inside of your business to strategize your target market list based on your publicity goals. Reaching the right people requires a proven and repeatable strategy.

  • Phase 2

    Creating your brand story can sometimes feel frustrating and awkward. This is where we come in - putting all the pieces of the puzzle together to create a compelling and eye-catching brand story that wows journalists, producers, and editors. And of course, your approval is needed. We’ll start outreach, once you’ve approved your perfectly crafted brand story.

  • Phase 3

    While you’re moving in your zone of genius, we’re making things happen in the background through our proactive media pitching, and strategically increasing your website traffic and social media presence.

  • Phase 4

    Transparency is a big part of our process. It’s one reason why we make it a priority to share weekly/bi-weekly press updates, a monthly status report, and a final wrap report.
